The worst part about growing up financially disadvantaged isn't the going without as much as it is the feeling unworthy of the thing. 

It all stacks up like the bottom line in your head at the grocery store and nobody warns you. The confusion, as it grows, is unsettling.

Over time you develop weird relationships with authority that you can't understand because you just want to give. 

There's no one to even blame, truly. Everyone sort of just has there own song to sing. 

Maybe one should learn what tender, love, and care really means.


Once it all compounds, much like my own disinterest in the whole "circumstance," and you move around with so many other people all "in it,"

you quickly learn that who you surround yourself with is everything. I'm not sure how you learn to deal with levels of distrust

you develop in literally everything and everyone. When I watch the way people move around me it's as if the motion

itself occurs like my death depends on it. The goal post is forever moving.


This is all just to heal myself, really. It seems like eveyrthing has been taken from me over these years. Nothing muuch to grow on.

Except for writing. It must be that the gods like to see me write. Surely it's true. Life has given me so much to say. To express.

While taking everything I thought I loved or needed, every door closed. Fate would then have it that I sit in it

with nothing else than my own conciousness and the ability to convey it in a manner

that is indelible in it's own right. I take no credit for it. God is the Creditor.


Truth is, I have no idea what I'm doing and never have. I wonder if that's a human thing to say. Isolation will do this to

one of us. I was one person and then someone came into my life and it's as though I woke up one day and

had to remember who I was before. I grow and I realize that none of it even matters except for where

I'm going and my daughter. I never came here to hurt anyone or cause pain or harm,

and can't continue to believe this all can't be made into a thing that is good.


My mission statement is to discontinue the current narrative which, in all due respect, is disorder and chaos. Nonsense.

The taking way too long to not do enough when I know the whole world exists in a way 

that I can hold in the palm of my hand and give it back to itself desists. 

While this path, however narrowly traveled, is meek...

I know it has been walked before. So who sent you?
