Some find theirs at the crossroads with Baron Samedi,
others somewhere in the lines of a peaceful treaty.
I found my enlightenment where souls distill,
the point where God's Plan crosses Free Will.

Now abandoned the cauldron, for fearful the witch
of the lines of the salt and the spiritual gift.
They are conveyed about in a bottle, a comfortable hearse,
reflecting energetic waste from the bottom of the universe.

Lacking the grip to take lives with strangulation,
so far harder they try with triangulation,
binding in darkness and claiming the night is true,
while cursing at the light that is shining through.

Spitefully, sodium only holds what agrees to be held,
and a curse is a letter freely written unto itself.
There is neither jar nor thread where souls distill,
only a violet flame burning, the plan finding the will.
